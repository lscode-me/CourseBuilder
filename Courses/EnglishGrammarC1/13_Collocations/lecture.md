## 1. What Are Collocations?

### 1.1 Definition

**Collocation** = words that naturally go together.

```
NATURAL (collocation):
"make a decision" ✓
"heavy rain" ✓
"commit a crime" ✓

UNNATURAL (not collocation):
"do a decision" ✗
"strong rain" ✗
"make a crime" ✗
```

### 1.2 Why They Matter

Native speakers don't say things "incorrectly" — they say them unnaturally.

```
CORRECT BUT UNNATURAL:
"The rain was very strong."

NATURAL:
"The rain was very heavy." / "It was raining heavily."
```

## 2. Make vs Do

### 2.1 General Patterns

**MAKE:** Creating, producing, causing
**DO:** Activities, tasks, actions

### 2.2 MAKE Collocations

```
DECISIONS/PLANS:
make a decision, make a choice, make plans, 
make arrangements, make a suggestion

COMMUNICATION:
make a phone call, make a complaint, make a comment,
make a promise, make an excuse, make a point

FOOD:
make breakfast/lunch/dinner, make coffee/tea,
make a meal, make a cake

MONEY:
make money, make a profit, make a living

CAUSE:
make a mistake, make an effort, make progress,
make noise, make trouble, make a difference

OTHER:
make friends, make an appointment, make an exception,
make sense, make sure, make an attempt
```

### 2.3 DO Collocations

```
WORK/TASKS:
do work, do homework, do housework, do business,
do research, do a job, do exercise

GENERAL ACTIVITIES:
do the shopping, do the cooking, do the cleaning,
do the washing, do the dishes, do the laundry

HARM:
do damage, do harm, do good

PERFORMANCE:
do well/badly, do your best, do someone a favor

OTHER:
do nothing, do something, do anything
```

### 2.4 Common Errors

```
❌ "do a mistake" → ✓ "make a mistake"
❌ "make homework" → ✓ "do homework"
❌ "do a decision" → ✓ "make a decision"
❌ "make business" → ✓ "do business"
```

## 3. Have vs Take

### 3.1 HAVE Collocations

```
MEALS:
have breakfast/lunch/dinner, have a meal, have a snack

DRINKS:
have coffee/tea, have a drink, have a beer

EXPERIENCES:
have fun, have a good time, have a great time,
have problems, have difficulty, have trouble

COMMUNICATION:
have a conversation, have a chat, have a talk,
have an argument, have a discussion

EVENTS:
have a meeting, have a party, have a lesson

OTHER:
have a look, have a rest, have a bath/shower,
have an idea, have an effect, have a chance
```

### 3.2 TAKE Collocations

```
TRANSPORT:
take a bus/train/taxi/plane, take a flight

TIME:
take time, take (10 minutes), take a while, take long

ACTION:
take action, take steps, take measures, take a risk

RESPONSIBILITY:
take responsibility, take care (of), take charge

PHOTOS/NOTES:
take a photo, take a picture, take notes

TESTS:
take an exam/test, take a course

OTHER:
take a break, take a walk, take a seat, take place,
take part, take advice, take notice
```

### 3.3 Have vs Take

```
BrE "have a bath" / AmE "take a bath"
BrE "have a rest" / AmE "take a rest"
BrE "have a shower" / AmE "take a shower"
```

## 5. Adjective + Noun Collocations

### 5.1 Common Patterns

```
HEAVY: heavy rain, heavy traffic, heavy smoker
STRONG: strong coffee, strong wind, strong opinion
HIGH: high temperature, high speed, high quality
DEEP: deep sleep, deep breath, deep trouble
FAST: fast food, fast asleep, fast lane
```

### 5.2 Russian Interference Examples

```
❌ "strong rain" → ✓ "heavy rain"
❌ "high man" → ✓ "tall man"
❌ "big mistake" (sometimes) → ✓ "serious mistake"
❌ "strong tea" (OK in BrE!) but "heavy smoker" not "strong smoker"
```

## 6. Dependent Prepositions

### 6.1 Adjective + Preposition

```
afraid OF        "She's afraid of spiders."
interested IN    "I'm interested in history."
good AT          "He's good at math."
responsible FOR  "Who's responsible for this?"
similar TO       "This is similar to that."
different FROM   "It's different from mine."
proud OF         "I'm proud of you."
tired OF         "I'm tired of waiting."
worried ABOUT    "I'm worried about the exam."
angry WITH/AT    "She was angry with him."
```

### 6.2 Verb + Preposition

```
depend ON        "It depends on the weather."
believe IN       "I believe in you."
belong TO        "This belongs to me."
consist OF       "The team consists of 10 people."
listen TO        "Listen to me."
wait FOR         "Wait for me."
look FOR         "I'm looking for my keys."
look AT          "Look at this!"
think ABOUT/OF   "What do you think about it?"
agree WITH       "I agree with you."
apologize FOR    "I apologize for being late."
```

### 6.3 Common Russian Interference

```
❌ depend FROM (зависеть от) → ✓ depend ON
❌ interested OF → ✓ interested IN
❌ listen (without TO) → ✓ listen TO
❌ wait (without FOR) → ✓ wait FOR
❌ marry WITH → ✓ marry (no preposition!)
❌ discuss ABOUT → ✓ discuss (no preposition!)
❌ explain ME → ✓ explain TO me
❌ enter INTO the room → ✓ enter the room
```

## 7. Phrasal Verb Collocations

### 7.1 Common Combinations

```
LOOK: look up (in dictionary), look after (care for),
      look forward to, look into (investigate)

GET: get up, get on (with), get over, get through,
     get along (with), get rid of

TAKE: take off, take up (hobby), take over,
      take after (resemble)

GIVE: give up, give in, give away, give back

PUT: put off (postpone), put up with (tolerate),
     put on, put away
```

## 8. Fixed Expressions


### 8.2 Idioms with Collocations

## 9. How to Learn Collocations

### 9.1 Strategies

1. **Learn words in context**: Not "decision" but "make a decision"
2. **Use collocation dictionaries**: Oxford, Macmillan
3. **Read extensively**: Notice natural combinations
4. **Use corpora**: COCA, BNC to check combinations

### 9.2 Resources

- Oxford Collocations Dictionary
- Macmillan Collocations Dictionary
- ozdic.com (online)
- ludwig.guru

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- **Make vs Do**: Make = create/cause; Do = tasks/activities
- **Prepositions are not translatable**: Learn each verb/adjective with its preposition
- **Russian calques**: Avoid translating directly (depend FROM → ON)
- **Heavy/Strong**: Heavy rain, heavy traffic, strong wind, strong coffee
- **Learn chunks**: Not "decision" but "make a decision"
