## Answer Key

| # | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | D | "Water" as a generic concept takes zero article. "The water" would mean specific water. |
| 2 | C | Classification requires an indefinite article. "An" because "excellent" starts with a vowel sound. |
| 3 | C | THE signals shared knowledge. Both people know which door — the one in the room. |
| 4 | D | Rivers take THE. Countries without "republic/kingdom/states" take zero article. Mountains take zero article. |
| 5 | C | Abstract concepts used generically take zero article. "Love" here means love in general. |
| 6 | C | First mention of a singular countable noun = indefinite article A/AN. "A strange bird" — you don't know which one. |
| 7 | C | "In prison" = functioning as prisoner. "At the prison" = at the building (as visitor, worker, etc.) |
| 8 | C | Languages take zero article. We don't say "the Japanese" or "a Japanese" when referring to the language. |
| 9 | D | "Information" is uncountable. We can't say "an information." Correct: "some information" or "a piece of information." |
| 10 | A | Generic reference with plural nouns = zero article. "Dogs" means all dogs in general. |
| 11 | C | The phrase "on the table" specifies which book, making it identifiable to the listener → THE. |
| 12 | C | Russian has no articles, so speakers often add "the" to generic concepts, translating from Russian definite context. |
| 13 | D | "By car" (means of transport) is a fixed expression with zero article. Same as "by bus," "by train." |
| 14 | B | "A Mr. Johnson" means "a person called Mr. Johnson" — someone you don't know. It marks unfamiliarity. |
| 15 | D | Superlatives require THE definite article. "The best," "the most," "the first," etc. |
| 16 | A | "Have lunch" = meal as institution (zero article). "The lunch" = that specific lunch we ate. |
| 17 | C | In formal/academic writing, "the + singular" can represent the whole class. "The tiger" = tigers as a species. |
| 18 | D | Should be "The President gave A speech" — "speech" is a singular countable noun, first mention, needs an article. |
| 19 | B | "A car" — you want any car, it's a first mention, and you're not specifying which one. Classic indefinite article use. |
| 20 | C | "The history OF Russia" (specifying phrase), "Russia" (country name = ∅), "university" (institution = ∅ in BrE). |
