## 1. Переход к Manager of Managers

### 1.1 Что меняется

**Engineering Manager → Director:**

| Aspect | EM | Director |
|--------|-----|----------|
| Direct reports | ICs | Managers (+ some ICs) |
| Scope | 1 team | Multiple teams |
| Focus | Execution | Strategy + Systems |
| Impact | Team level | Org level |
| Decision making | Tactical | Strategic |
| People work | Direct | Through others |

### 1.2 The Hard Part

> **💡 Key Insight:**  
> Самое сложное — перестать делать работу менеджера первой линии. Ваша работа теперь — развивать менеджеров.

**Common mistakes:**
```

❌ Micromanaging ваших менеджеров
❌ Solving проблемы за них
❌ Skip-level без awareness менеджера
❌ Принятие решений, которые должны принимать они
❌ Бесконечные 1-on-1 со всеми ICs

```

### 1.3 New Responsibilities

```

Building Leaders:

- Hire и develop менеджеров
- Coach и mentor
- Succession planning

Creating Systems:

- Processes для scale
- Communication mechanisms
- Decision frameworks

Strategic Work:

- Org design
- Headcount planning
- Cross-org initiatives

Executive Interface:

- Represent eng to business
- Budget discussions
- Strategic planning

```

## 2. Managing Managers

### 2.1 Different 1-on-1

**With ICs:**
```

- Career development
- Technical challenges
- Blocking issues
- Feedback on work

```

**With Managers:**
```

- Their team's health
- Their development as leaders
- Organizational challenges
- Strategy alignment
- Their struggles and growth

```

### 2.2 Questions для менеджеров

```

Team Health:

- "How is your team doing?"
- "Who's struggling? Who's excelling?"
- "Any flight risks?"
- "What's morale like?"

Their Leadership:

- "What's challenging you as a manager?"
- "What did you learn this week?"
- "Where do you need help?"
- "What decision was hardest?"

Strategy:

- "Are we working on the right things?"
- "What should we stop doing?"
- "What's blocking your team's success?"

```

### 2.3 Coaching Managers

**Coaching vs Directing:**
```

Directing: "Do X"
Coaching: "What options do you see? What would happen if...?"

```

**When to direct:**

- Crisis situations
- Clear mistakes being made
- Time-sensitive decisions

**When to coach:**

- Development opportunity
- They can figure it out
- Building judgment

**Coaching framework:**
```

1. Ask about the situation
2. Ask what they've considered
3. Ask about trade-offs they see
4. Share perspective (if asked or critical)
5. Let them decide
6. Debrief later: "What did you learn?"

```

### 2.4 Giving Managers Feedback

**On their team:**
```

"I noticed your team's velocity dropped. 
What's your read on that?"

```

**On their leadership:**
```

"In that meeting, you jumped to solution quickly.
What would happen if you asked more questions first?"

```

**On their growth:**
```

"You're strong at execution. 
For next level, you'll need more strategic thinking.
What opportunities do you see to develop that?"

```

## 3. Skip-Level Relationships

### 3.1 Purpose of Skip-Levels

```

- Unfiltered view of org
- Culture and morale check
- Manager feedback (carefully!)
- Career conversations
- Build trust across levels

```

### 3.2 How to Do Skip-Levels

**Frequency:**

- Monthly or quarterly rotation
- Meet each person 1-2x per year minimum

**Format:**
```

NOT:

- Performance review
- Complaint session
- Undermining manager

YES:

- "How are things going?"
- "What's working well on your team?"
- "What would make your work better?"
- "Any feedback for me?"

```

### 3.3 Handling Manager Complaints

**When IC complains about their manager:**

```

1. Listen without reacting
2. Ask clarifying questions
3. Don't commit to action
4. "Thanks for sharing, let me think about this"
5. Verify with other data points
6. If pattern: address with manager
7. Never throw manager under the bus

```

## 4. Organizational Health

### 4.1 Indicators to Track

**Team Health:**
```

- Engagement scores
- Turnover rate
- Promotion rate
- Internal mobility
- 1-on-1 coverage

```

**Delivery Health:**
```

- Velocity trends
- Lead time
- Quality metrics
- Incident rates
- Technical debt

```

**Manager Health:**
```

- Manager satisfaction
- Span of control
- Manager turnover
- Skip-level feedback

```

### 4.2 Org Review

**Regular cadence (monthly/quarterly):**
```

1. Review metrics
2. Discuss each team
3. Identify interventions needed
4. Resource allocation decisions
5. Manager development plans

```

### 4.3 Intervention Patterns

**Team struggling:**
```

- Is it manager issue or team issue?
- What support does manager need?
- Do we need to add resources?
- Is org design the problem?

```

**Manager struggling:**
```

- What's the root cause?
- Can they grow into it?
- What support can we provide?
- Is it the right fit?

```

## 5. Executive Communication

### 5.1 Управление вверх

**What execs need:**
```

- Business impact, not technical details
- Clear asks and recommendations
- Risks and mitigations
- Progress against goals
- No surprises

```

### 5.2 Executive Presence

**Components:**
```

Clarity:

- Concise communication
- Bottom line up front
- Structured thinking

Confidence:

- Speak with conviction
- Own your area
- Admit what you don't know

Composure:

- Stay calm under pressure
- Handle tough questions
- Don't get defensive

```

### 5.3 Влияние на decisions

```

1. Understand the priorities of execs
2. Frame in terms of business value
3. Come with data and options
4. Make clear recommendation
5. Show you've thought through risks
6. Be willing to commit to outcomes

```

## 6. Strategic Thinking

### 6.1 Time Horizon Shift

**EM:** Weeks to months
**Director:** Quarters to year
**VP:** 1-3 years
**C-level:** 3-5+ years

### 6.2 Strategic Questions

```

For your org:

- Where should we be in 1 year? 3 years?
- What capabilities do we need to build?
- What should we stop doing?
- What's the biggest risk to our success?
- How does eng support business strategy?

```

### 6.3 Portfolio Thinking

**Balance:**
```

- Keep the lights on (maintenance)
- Improve existing (enhancement)
- Build new (innovation)
- Reduce debt (investment)

```

**Typical allocation:**
```

60% - Core product work
20% - Tech debt / Infrastructure
10% - Innovation / Experimentation
10% - Buffer / Unplanned

```

## 7. Organizational Design

### 7.1 Conway's Law

```

"Organizations design systems that mirror 
their communication structure."

```

**Implication:**
Your org structure affects your architecture.
Design org for the architecture you want.

### 7.2 Team Design Principles

```

- Teams own products/services, not projects
- Minimize dependencies between teams
- Clear interfaces and contracts
- Team size 5-9 people
- Align teams with business domains

```

### 7.3 Reorgs

**When to reorg:**
```

- Strategy changed significantly
- Current structure blocking goals
- Teams too large or too small
- Dependencies causing friction

```

**How to reorg:**
```

1. Define desired outcomes
2. Design new structure
3. Plan transition
4. Communicate clearly (why, what, when)
5. Execute quickly
6. Support through transition
7. Don't reorg too often

```

## 8. Building Your Leadership Team

### 8.1 Hiring Senior Leaders

**What to look for:**
```

- Track record of building teams
- Strategic thinking
- Executive presence
- Cultural fit + culture add
- Complements your weaknesses

```

### 8.2 Developing Leadership Pipeline

```

1. Identify high-potentials early
2. Give stretch opportunities
3. Coaching and mentoring
4. External development (conferences, coaching)
5. Succession planning

```

### 8.3 Your Leadership Team Dynamics

```

- Regular leadership syncs
- Shared goals and metrics
- Healthy debate culture
- Support each other publicly
- Disagree and commit

```

## 9. Self-Care at Senior Levels

### 9.1 Challenges

```

- More responsibility, less control
- Political complexity
- Loneliness at the top
- Visibility of failures
- Work-life boundaries blur

```

### 9.2 Strategies

```

- Executive coach
- Peer network (outside company)
- Protected personal time
- Delegation discipline
- Regular reflection

```

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- **Через других:** Ваш impact теперь через менеджеров, не напрямую

- **Coach, don't direct:** Развивайте judgment менеджеров

- **Systems thinking:** Создавайте процессы для scale

- **Executive presence:** Ясность, уверенность, composure

- **Long-term view:** Think quarters and years, not weeks
