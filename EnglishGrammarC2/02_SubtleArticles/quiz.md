## Answer Key

| # | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | B | "Climate change" is a generic concept, requiring zero article. |
| 2 | B | "The music" is specified by "of the Baroque period" — post-modification requires THE. |
| 3 | C | "The screen" — associative reference to the screen of the laptop mentioned. |
| 4 | A | In formal academic writing, "The + singular" is acceptable for generic statements. |
| 5 | B | "The poor" = poor people (THE + adjective pattern). "Government" = "the government" (unique in context). |
| 6 | B | "The knowledge" — specified by the relative clause "she gained". |
| 7 | C | "Smartphones" (plural, zero article) is the standard generic form. |
| 8 | B | "Happiness" is abstract/generic — zero article for generic concepts. |
| 9 | C | "Research" (zero article for generic) and "students" (zero article for generic category). |
| 10 | B | "The water" refers to specific water previously discussed — shared knowledge. |
| 11 | C | "The science has advanced" is wrong — generic science needs zero article. |
| 12 | B | THE signals shared knowledge — both speaker and listener know which station. |
| 13 | B | "The French" — THE + nationality adjective = the people of that nationality. |
| 14 | B | "The unconditional love" — pre-modification with "unconditional" + "of a parent" = specific. |
| 15 | C | "The life" is a typical Russian error — generic "life" needs zero article. |
| 16 | B | British English: "in hospital" (function/institution). American English: "in the hospital". |
| 17 | B | "The technology" — specified by "in the 21st century". |
| 18 | C | "A good wine" = a good type/example of wine. A + uncountable = type. |
| 19 | B | "A man" introduces new information — the listener doesn't know which man. |
| 20 | A | All nouns are properly specified: "The analysis" (specific), "the data" (specific), "the relationship" (specific), "the variables" (specific). |
